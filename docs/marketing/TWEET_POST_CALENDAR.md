## Rules

1. **One original tweet per day** at 3-4pm SAST (9-10am EST). That's it. No second post, no thread.
2. **No CTAs, no links, no "Full episode on Auditure."** The bio handles the funnel.
3. **No video until the format is fixed.** Current format gets 9-second average watch time and 0.5% completion. Don't post more of the same.
4. **Sound like a person, not a brand.** Write the tweet like it's a reply to someone's post — casual, opinionated, specific.
5. **No self-promo follow-up replies.** When a reply hits big, do NOT reply to yourself with "we covered this on Auditure." The algorithm kills it every time.
6. **Quote tweet your own winning replies.** When a reply crosses 10K impressions, quote tweet it within 2-3 hours with an additional take. This moves reply impressions onto your profile.
7. **The tweet should match the day's episode** from the Episode Snippets Calendar — same book, but the tweet stands alone as content.

## When to Bring Video Back

Video is paused until a new format is tested. The current format (book cover + AI audio + captions) gets 9-second average watch time. Before reintroducing video:

1. **New format requirement:** First frame must be a text hook on screen (not a book cover). Example: "Herbert wrote Dune Messiah as a trap for his own readers." Then audio kicks in.
2. **Length:** Under 30 seconds, not 2-4 minutes.
3. **Test method:** Make 3 videos with the new format. Post them as replies to your own takes that are getting traction — not as standalone posts.
4. **Success threshold:** Average watch time above 20 seconds AND completion rate above 5%.
5. **If it passes:** Reintroduce video as a reply to your own original take (not as the original post itself). The text take drives impressions, the video reply gives curious people more.
6. **If it fails:** Video is not your Twitter format right now. Focus on text.

## What Success Looks Like

The old template posts got 4-43 impressions. If the new format consistently gets:
- **100-500 impressions:** Progress — the algorithm is starting to show your originals
- **500-2K impressions:** Working — your follower base is growing and engaging
- **2K+:** The take format has product-market fit on your profile

Track this weekly. If after 2 weeks the new format still gets <100 impressions, the issue is follower count, not content — and the only lever is continuing to grow through replies until you have enough of a base for originals to distribute.
