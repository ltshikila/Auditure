## Phase 3: POST-LAUNCH (Week 3+)
**Goal:** Sustain growth, build community, drive retention

### Content Calendar (3-5 tweets per week)

**Monday -- Build in public update**
- Weekly metrics: downloads, episodes generated, new features shipping
- "Last week: 47 episodes generated. Most popular book: Atomic Habits. Most popular format: Debate."

**Wednesday -- Product content**
- Feature highlights with screen recordings
- Before/after comparisons
- User-generated episode clips (with permission)

**Friday -- Engagement / thought leadership**
- Book recommendations + "imagine this as a podcast"
- AI audio industry commentary
- Community highlights ("Check out what @user created this week")

### Growth Tactics

1. **Episode clips as content**
   - Post 30-60 second clips of AI-generated episodes
   - "Here's what happens when two AI hosts debate The 48 Laws of Power"
   - Audio content performs well and is unique -- nobody else is posting this

2. **User-generated content**
   - RT and quote-tweet users who share their episodes
   - Feature "Episode of the Week" from the community
   - This creates a flywheel: users create > share > new users discover

3. **Collaborations**
   - Reach out to book reviewers / bookstagram / booktok creators
   - Offer them free Pro access to create content
   - "I gave @bookreviewr early access to Auditure. Here's what she created."

4. **Trending topic hijacking**
   - When a book trends (Oprah's book club, viral TikTok book, etc.), create an episode from it and post the clip
   - "Everyone's talking about [trending book]. Here's what it sounds like as an AI debate podcast."

5. **Thread series**
   - Weekly "Book > Podcast" thread: pick a popular book, generate an episode, share the clip
   - "Solo Dev Diaries" thread: share technical challenges and wins
   - These become recurring content people follow for

### Metrics to Track
- Follower growth rate (target: 100 followers in first month)
- Engagement rate per tweet (aim for >2%)
- Click-through to Play Store link
- Which content type drives the most profile visits
- DM conversations that convert to downloads

## Content Rules
1. Never use more than 2 hashtags per tweet
2. No emojis in every tweet -- use sparingly
3. Video/audio clips > images > text-only (algorithm preference)
4. Reply to every comment in the first hour
5. Don't sell in every tweet -- 80% value, 20% CTA
6. Be human, not corporate. "We" is fine but "I" is better for build-in-public
7. Post between 9am-12pm EST for maximum reach
