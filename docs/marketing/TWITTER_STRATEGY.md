### Pre-Viral Detection Model (Engagement Ratio Analysis)

Like a trader reading volume before a breakout, we track engagement ratios to detect posts that are about to go viral — and reply before they do.

#### Baseline: Normal Twitter Engagement Ratios
| Metric | Normal Range | Formula |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Like Ratio | 0.3% - 1.0% | likes / views × 100 |
| RT Ratio | 0.03% - 0.10% | retweets / views × 100 |
| Bookmark Ratio | 0.02% - 0.05% | bookmarks / views × 100 |
| Reply Ratio | 0.05% - 0.20% | replies / views × 100 |

#### Signal: High Engagement Ratio (Pre-Viral Indicator)
When a post's engagement ratios are **2-5x above baseline**, the post is accumulating engagement faster than the algorithm is distributing it. This is the window — the post is about to be pushed to Explore/For You feeds.

| Metric | Pre-Viral Range | Signal Strength |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| Like Ratio | >2.0% | Strong — people are actively endorsing |
| RT Ratio | >0.15% | Strong — people are sharing to their own audience |
| Bookmark Ratio | >0.08% | Very strong — people saving = high perceived value |
| Reply Ratio | <0.05% (LOW) | Opportunity — engagement is high but the conversation is empty, your reply fills the gap |

**The golden signal: High likes + high bookmarks + low replies.** This means people love the post but nobody has said anything worth reading yet. Your reply becomes the top reply as the post scales.

#### Case Study #1: Reminders of Him (Global Box Office) — Mar 14-15, 2026

**At time of reply (1:32 AM, ~9 hrs after post):**
| Metric | Value | Ratio | vs. Baseline |
|--------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Views | 16,500 | — | — |
| Likes | 399 | 2.42% | **4.8x above normal** |
| RTs | 29 | 0.18% | **3.0x above normal** |
| Bookmarks | 17 | 0.10% | **2.5x above normal** |
| Replies | 7 | 0.04% | **Below normal (opportunity)** |

**Verdict:** All three engagement signals above 2x baseline. Reply ratio below normal. Classic pre-viral setup.

**Result (next day, ~3:12 PM):**
| Metric | Before | After | Growth |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Parent views | 16,500 | 1,800,000 | **109x** |
| Parent likes | 399 | 1,600 | 4x |
| Parent RTs | 29 | 127 | 4.4x |
| Our reply views | 0 | 88,100 | — |
| Reply reach vs parent | — | 4.9% | — |

**Key stat:** Our reply captured **4.9% of the parent's total reach** by being early. A reply posted after the blowup would capture <0.1%.

#### Decision Framework: Should I Reply?

**GREEN — Reply immediately (high conviction):**
- Like ratio >2.0% AND reply count <10
- Post is <12 hrs old
- From an account with >10K followers
- Topic is in our lane (books, adaptations, entertainment, tech)
- Expected outcome: if the post goes viral, your reply rides the wave

**YELLOW — Reply if you have a strong take:**
- Like ratio 1.0-2.0%
- Post is <6 hrs old
- Moderate reply count (10-30)
- Expected outcome: decent visibility, not guaranteed blowup

**RED — Skip or deprioritize:**
- Like ratio <1.0% (normal engagement, won't break out)
- Post is >24 hrs old (already peaked or stalled)
- Reply count >50 (your reply will be buried)
- Topic is outside our lanes

#### Late Reply Exception (The Overnight Window)
A post can still be worth replying to 6-12 hours after posting IF:
- It was posted in US evening/night hours (low initial distribution)
- Engagement ratios are elevated (>2x baseline)
- The post will hit US morning feeds the next day (second distribution wave)
- This is what happened with Reminders of Him — posted 4:33 PM, we replied 1:32 AM, it exploded the next morning when US woke up

**Probability estimate based on observed data:**
- Pre-viral signal detected + early reply = **~15-25% chance** the post breaks 500K+ views (and your reply gets 10K+ views)
- Same post without pre-viral signals = **<2% chance** of breakout
- Replying after breakout (>500K views) = **<5% chance** your reply gets meaningful visibility

*Note: These are working estimates based on limited data. Update with each new case study.*

## Episode Clip Content Strategy (Primary Content)

Episode clips are the main content type. Every day, post a 30-60 second screen recording of an episode playing in the app (book cover, player, captions visible). This is the unfair advantage -- no one else on Twitter is posting AI-generated podcast debates about popular books.

### Post Template

> [Hook question or provocative statement about the book's topic]
>
> From: [Book Name] by [Author]
> Full episode on Auditure
>
> [video]

**Rules:**
- Hook line is about the *idea*, not the app -- make people curious enough to press play
- "Full episode on Auditure" is the only branding -- doubles as a soft CTA that tells people there's more to hear
- Pick the most interesting 30-60 seconds: moments where hosts disagree, say something surprising, or challenge conventional wisdom
- One clip per day, posted at 3-4pm SAST (9-10am EST)
- Screen record the player screen with book cover and captions visible

### Book Selection Algorithm

Select books each week using this priority system. A good week has a mix from multiple categories.

**Tier 1: Culturally Trending (highest priority -- ride existing attention)**
- Books with active TV/film adaptations (e.g., Bridgerton, House of the Dragon, The Housemaid)
- Books trending on BookTok/BookTube/Bookstagram
- Books by authors in the news
- Check: Google Trends, Twitter trending, Netflix/streaming new releases, #BookTok

**Tier 2: Evergreen Classics (reliable engagement -- massive existing audiences)**
- Self-improvement: Atomic Habits, 48 Laws of Power, Rich Dad Poor Dad, Think and Grow Rich
- Psychology: The Laws of Human Nature, Thinking Fast and Slow, The Psychology of Money
- Philosophy: The Art of War, Meditations, Man's Search for Meaning
- Business: Zero to One, The Lean Startup, Good to Great

**Tier 3: Controversial / Debate-Friendly (highest share potential)**
- Books with polarizing ideas that naturally spark debate
- Books where the "debate" format shines because there are genuinely two sides
- e.g., Outliers (10,000 hour rule), The Secret, Think and Grow Rich (manifestation vs. action)

**Tier 4: Niche Community Books (targeted reach)**
- Tech/AI: books that resonate with the #buildinpublic and AI audience
- Finance: books the crypto/investing Twitter audience engages with
- Stoicism: books the Ryan Holiday / Daily Stoic audience follows

**Repeat Rule:** A book can be featured across multiple weeks if:
- Its adaptation releases new episodes (e.g., weekly TV drops)
- A previous clip performed well (double down on what works)
- Different chapters/topics from the same book offer fresh angles
